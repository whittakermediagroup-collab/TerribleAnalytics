05 — Built to Repeat

Content Systems Now Live

The numbers above came from repeatable series, not one-off posts. Each of these is now a standing franchise with its own format, cadence, and shot list, which means next month's calendar is already structured.

Not So Terrible Deals

Monthly deal series shot in-store, batch-produced in a single shoot day. July featured Aquafina, Michelob ULTRA, Twisted Tea, Stella Artois, HI-CHEW, and Monster across all platforms.

Terrible Tuesday

Weekly app-exclusive deal drop driving T+ Rewards opens. Same spot, new deal, every week. Builds a habit loop between social and the app.

#TerribleArt

Mural series with partner brands. The Red Bull wall and the FRE x Riley Herbst race-day drop both landed this month, turning store locations into destinations.

100 Days of Summer Support

Weekly social amplification of the summer rewards program, keeping daily fuel discounts and BOGO offers in front of the audience all season.

Moment Marketing

Free Frazil Friday, fireworks stands, the Car Wash Job Fair. Real things happening at real locations, covered fast.

The Announcement Playbook

Primm proved the formula: platform-native versions, media tagging, creator amplification, day-of follow-up. Ready to deploy for the next big brand moment.
